## Scope

reproducible failure-injection procedures and operator-facing symptoms.

## Non-goals

- Starting destructive tests before T08 makes E2 repeatable and validates the safety boundary.
- Performance conclusions or broad ecosystem support claims.
- Accepting silent corruption or data loss as a test waiver.

## Acceptance criteria

- FN-01–FN-30 and REL-F01–REL-F15 have dated results; failures have severity and tracking.
- No open S1 data-loss or silent-corruption result remains.
- Every data-bearing failure case records an integrity result and its permitted unflushed-data scope.
- Failure procedures restore E2 to its documented healthy baseline for two consecutive runs.
- Operational symptoms link to actual reports without copying logs into `DEPLOY.md`.
- The nightly workflow has three executions and records rather than hides flaky behavior.
