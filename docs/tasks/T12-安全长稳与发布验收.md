# T12: Security, soak, and release acceptance

## Scope

Complete security/multi-tenant evidence, 72-hour resource and integrity testing, operational and
negative-path validation, waiver review, and the evidence-backed release decision.

## Non-goals

- Starting before T08–T11 complete or before S1/S2 defects are cleared.
- Switching the shared E3 environment to Kerberos in place.
- Declaring readiness from documentation or short functional runs alone.

## Acceptance criteria

- SEC-01–SEC-10 complete; credential-leak scans have zero secret matches; tenant isolation guidance
  is actionable and evidence-backed.
- SOAK-01 runs continuously for 72 hours with its integrity requirements; SOAK-02–SOAK-05 meet their
  resource and lifecycle thresholds without manual cleanup masking the result.
- OPS-01–OPS-10 and NEG-01–NEG-08 have dated results.
- No open S1/S2 issue remains. Every unmet gate has a reviewed waiver with scope and review trigger.
- The release-readiness decision links each gate to a report and does not repeat test steps.
