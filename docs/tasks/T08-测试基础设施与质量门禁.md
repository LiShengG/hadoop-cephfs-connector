# T08: Test infrastructure and quality gates

## Scope

Make E1/E2/E3 reproducible, add automated CI and quality gates, extend Hadoop contract coverage, and
verify artifact reproducibility without changing product behavior solely to satisfy infrastructure.

## Non-goals

- Reliability fault injection, performance benchmarking, and full ecosystem certification.
- Destructive disk discovery or operations outside the E2 device allowlist.
- Treating an existing manually built environment as repeatable provisioning.

## Acceptance criteria

- E2 and E3 can be provisioned or restored through tracked, idempotent entry points and validated by
  their read-only status commands.
- Required core contract definitions execute on E2 with differences recorded by test ID.
- Unit, cluster-gated, coverage, static-analysis, dependency/CVE, and artifact checks fail the build
  when their documented thresholds are not met.
- Pull-request and merge gates have three dated successful executions and meet their duration limits.
- Artifact checks cover reproducibility and native-library build-path contamination.
